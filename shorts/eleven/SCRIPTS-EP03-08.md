# 11:11: Episodes 3–8 (drafts for approval)

**The arc:** the seven-day countdown runs through Episode 7, then Episode 8 opens the next arc.

**The mystery that builds:**
- Kabir's voice notes save Mira.
- Then Kabir starts getting voice notes in *Mira's* voice, which save him.
- The messages come from the number of Arjun, Kabir's dead brother.
- Three years ago, on the old bridge at 11:11, Mira held a dying stranger's hand. That stranger was Arjun.

**Format:** every episode opens on a 2-second hook (a later line from the same episode, never the cliffhanger) and ends on a cliffhanger and the countdown card. Only Mira's and Kabir's voices are used, so no new voice costs. The **(Mira, voice note)** lines are her voice through the phone.

## Episode 3: "The 6:40 Train" · ends on FOUR DAYS LEFT
**HOOK:** KABIR: "You knew. Didn't you?"

1. MIRA: "He told me not to let him get on the train. So I didn't sleep."
2. MIRA: "6:30. Platform four. He was already there."
3. KABIR (surprised, smiling): "Mira? What are you doing here?"
4. MIRA: "I didn't have a plan. So I spilled my coffee… all over him."
5. KABIR (laughing, a little annoyed): "Okay. That's twice now. Are you doing this on purpose?"
6. MIRA (soft): "The doors closed. The train left without him. I didn't let go of his hand."
7. MIRA (low): "An hour later, every phone on the street buzzed at once."
8. MIRA (shaken): "The 6:40 train… never made it to the next station."
9. KABIR (quiet, staring at her): "You knew. Didn't you?"
10. MIRA: "I couldn't tell him. Not yet."
11. MIRA (uneasy): "That night, at 11:11… it wasn't my phone that lit up."
12. KABIR (barely a whisper): "Mira… why is your voice on my phone?"

**New art (about $0.30):** metro platform background; Kabir soaked in coffee, surprised; their hands holding (close-up); Kabir at night staring at his phone.

## Episode 4: "Her Voice" · ends on THREE DAYS LEFT
**HOOK:** MIRA (voice note): "Don't let her near the bridge."

1. MIRA: "He played it for me. My voice. Words I've never said."
2. MIRA (voice note, tired, scared): "Kabir, it's me. Mira. You don't know me yet… but you will. Please don't get on the 6:40 train."
3. KABIR (stunned): "I got that three nights ago. I thought it was a prank. Until today."
4. MIRA: "His messages were saving me. Mine were saving him."
5. MIRA (soft): "Somewhere out there… we were trying to save each other."
6. KABIR (a small smile): "So in the future… we know each other."
7. MIRA (shy, warm): "He said it like it was a good thing."
8. MIRA (low): "Then he played the newest one."
9. MIRA (voice note, crying, barely holding on): "Kabir. Friday night. Don't let her near the bridge."
10. MIRA (afraid): "Friday. Three days from now."
11. KABIR (firm, gentle): "Then I'm not leaving your side. Not for a minute."
12. MIRA (whisper): "That's what scared me. Because in seven days… he doesn't make it."

**New art (about $0.25):** rooftop at night with city lights; the two of them side by side on the rooftop ledge; Kabir holding his phone out to her.

## Episode 5: "The Brother" · ends on TWO DAYS LEFT
**HOOK:** KABIR: "He died on that bridge. At 11:11."

1. MIRA: "I had to know why his watch stopped. So I asked."
2. KABIR (holding it together): "My brother, Arjun. Three years ago. He died on the old bridge."
3. KABIR (quiet): "He died on that bridge. At 11:11."
4. MIRA (soft): "The same bridge. The same minute."
5. KABIR (sad half-smile): "He believed in signs. I never did."
6. MIRA (warm): "I held his hand. This time, he let me."
7. MIRA (unsettled): "Then he showed me a photo of Arjun."
8. MIRA (shaken, quiet): "Three years ago, on that bridge, I held a stranger's hand until the ambulance came."
9. MIRA (barely a whisper): "It was him. Arjun."
10. MIRA (low): "That night, 11:11."
11. KABIR (voice note, slow, heavy): "Now you know. You were there that night. That's why it has to be you."

**New art (about $0.30):** the old bridge at night in the rain; a photo of Arjun (a new face, handsome, like Kabir but softer); a flashback of Mira kneeling on the bridge holding a stranger's hand (drawn in faded tones); Kabir looking down at the photo.

## Episode 6: "Not Me" · ends on ONE DAY LEFT
**HOOK:** KABIR: "Mira… I've never recorded a single message."

1. MIRA: "One day left. I finally asked him what future him knows."
2. KABIR (confused, gentle): "Mira… I've never recorded a single message. Not one."
3. MIRA (stunned): "He showed me his phone. Nothing sent. Nothing at 11:11."
4. KABIR (uneasy, low): "And the ones in your voice… you didn't send those either. Did you?"
5. MIRA (afraid): "I didn't. I never had."
6. MIRA (low): "So who has been talking to us?"
7. MIRA (soft): "That night, we sat on his floor, my head on his shoulder. Waiting."
8. MIRA (whisper): "11:10. 11:11."
9. MIRA (frozen): "Both phones lit up. The same number."
10. KABIR (barely breathing): "That's… that's Arjun's number."

**New art (about $0.20):** Kabir's apartment at night; the two of them sitting on the floor, her head on his shoulder. The two phones lighting up are drawn in code.

## Episode 7: "Day Seven" · ends on the clock reading 11:12
**HOOK:** KABIR: "Whatever happens… don't let go."

1. MIRA (steady): "Day seven. We didn't leave each other's side."
2. MIRA (warm): "Coffee. The café. His laugh. Like there was no tomorrow."
3. MIRA (low): "Then the rain came. At eleven, his phone buzzed. Arjun's number."
4. KABIR (shaking): "It's… a voice note. From me."
5. KABIR (voice note, calm, sad): "If she's with you on the bridge, she lives. If she isn't… you do. Choose her."
6. MIRA (breaking): "He'd already chosen."
7. KABIR (gentle, firm): "Whatever happens… don't let go."
8. MIRA (fast, breathless): "11:10. The bridge. Headlights out of the rain."
9. MIRA (whisper): "He pushed me back. Just like the first time."
10. MIRA (shattered): "11:11."
11. *Silence. Rain. A long beat.*
12. MIRA (barely a whisper, disbelief): "Then… his watch started ticking."

**End card:** "11:12", then "TO BE CONTINUED". The watch's hands move for the first time in the series, drawn in code.

**New art (about $0.20):** Kabir pushing her clear on the bridge, lit by headlights; Mira on the wet ground, looking up. The bridge background comes from Episode 5.

## Episode 8: "11:12" · opens the next arc · ends on "NEW MESSAGE"
**HOOK:** MIRA (voice note, older): "Ask Kabir what really happened the night Arjun died."

1. MIRA (shaky): "11:12. He was breathing."
2. KABIR (weak, smiling): "Told you I don't make it. Guess I was wrong."
3. MIRA (laughing through tears): "I'd never laughed and cried at the same time before."
4. MIRA (soft): "For the first time in seven days, 11:11 passed… and nothing came."
5. MIRA (warm): "A week of nothing. Coffee. Rain. Him."
6. MIRA (uneasy): "Until the eighth night."
7. MIRA (low): "11:11. A new message. In a voice I knew too well."
8. MIRA (voice note, calm, older, tired): "Hey. Don't freak out. It's me… you. Ten years from now."
9. MIRA (frozen): "My own voice."
10. MIRA (voice note, quiet, serious): "Ask Kabir what really happened the night Arjun died. He wasn't at home."

**New art (about $0.20):** Kabir hurt but smiling, lying in the rain with Mira holding him; a happy café two-shot for the "week of nothing" montage.

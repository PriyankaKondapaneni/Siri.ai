# 47 Paper Planes — series bible

A 30-part romantic serial for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Every episode is about 60 seconds, vertical
(1080×1920), and ends on a hook into the next. Clean, warm, all ages.

**Logline.** Two strangers whose windows face each other across a narrow alley fall in love through paper planes:
she writes, he only ever answers in drawings. When she finally asks to hear his voice, the answer changes how she
listens to everyone.

**The hook that runs through every episode:** *"She sent him forty-seven paper planes before she ever heard his
voice."* Each episode is a numbered stretch of the planes (a postal-stamp counter on screen, PLANE Nº 1 … 100),
so a viewer always knows where they are and how close the 47th is.

## Look and sound

- **Style:** whimsical, hand-made paper collage. Kraft and cream papers with torn edges, cut-out characters with a
  white paper border and a soft shadow, ink lines that "boil" like hand-drawn animation, washi tape, postage stamps,
  ransom-note title letters, pencil doodles that draw themselves. Movement steps on twos like stop motion.
- **Palette:** night navy and ink, lamp-light amber, dusty rose, paper cream, a single coral accent for the planes.
- **Voice:** one narrator (Fish Audio *Sarah*, drama-3) telling it like a secret. Characters never speak aloud:
  Leo is deaf, and the show keeps faith with that. His voice is his drawings, later his signs.
- **Music:** an original music-box and pizzicato theme ("the plane theme") that every episode varies; it drops out
  for the cliffhanger.

## Characters

- **Mira** (22). Fifth floor, east building. Works the early shift at a corner bakery. Funny when nervous, writes
  everything down. Dark bob, mustard cardigan, ink-stained fingers.
- **Leo** (24). Fourth floor, west building. Illustrator who draws for the local paper. Deaf since birth; signs
  (ASL), reads lips, never needed to speak to be understood. Messy hair, navy sweater, a pencil behind his ear.
- **Mrs. Ruiz** (70s). Ground floor, tends the alley's pots of tomatoes, sees everything, matchmaker. Her cat is
  Biscuit.
- **Nina** (19). Leo's younger sister, hearing, fluent in sign, blunt, on Mira's side.
